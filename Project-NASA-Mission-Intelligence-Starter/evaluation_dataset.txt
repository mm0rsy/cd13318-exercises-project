# NASA Mission Intelligence — Evaluation Dataset
# Format: one question per line. Lines starting with # are comments.
# Categories covered: overview, emergency, disaster_analysis, crew, technical, timeline

# --- Overview ---

What was the overall goal of the Apollo 13 mission before the accident occurred?
What was the Challenger mission STS-51L intended to accomplish?

# --- Emergency / Crisis ---

What warning signs or anomalies were present before the Challenger disaster occurred?

# --- Disaster Analysis ---

What technical failure caused the Space Shuttle Challenger to break apart after launch?

# --- Crew ---
Who were the crew members of Apollo 11 and what were their roles?
Who were the astronauts aboard Apollo 13 and how did they survive the crisis?

# --- Technical ---
How did Mission Control and the Apollo 13 crew work together to return the spacecraft safely to Earth?
What propulsion and navigation procedures were used during the Apollo 11 lunar descent and ascent?

# --- Timeline ---
